    Power off, but the schedule says ON? (unplanned outage)
    Short-notice unplanned outages — emergency repair works, equipment damage, wartime grid events — are tied to your exact address, not to your queue, so they never show up in the schedule above. DTEK publishes them per address: open dtek-oem.com.ua/ua/shutdowns, enter your address, and look for the red notice («За вашою адресою в даний момент відсутня електроенергія…») with the cause and the estimated restore time. Citywide emergency announcements from DTEK appear directly on this page as a red banner when the mirror captures them. If your power is off with no banner, the DTEK site above is the fastest check.
    Copy the two times from the DTEK notice and the app will track the recovery with a live countdown. Stored on this device only; cleared automatically the next day.
